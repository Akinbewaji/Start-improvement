An interactive, gamified onboarding adventure and initiation trial built for [start.ng](https://start.ng). Instead of a standard corporate form, users navigate a **5-waypoint Quest Map**, awaken their **Character Class**, experience **live UI theme shifting**, and test their reflexes in an arcade mini-game.

---

## 💡 The  Why: Why Whimsical Adventure Onboarding Works

### 1. The IKEA Effect & Psychological Ownership
Standard forms generate zero emotional attachment. When users actively choose a character archetype (e.g., **Code Sorcerer** vs. **Pixel Alchemist**) and tune their avatar crown, hairstyle, and relics, they co-create their persona. This psychological ownership transforms a passive registrant into an emotionally invested community member.

### 2. Radical Reduction of Cognitive Friction
Filling out text inputs feels like administrative paperwork. Traversing an **Origin Gate** across a visual **Quest Map** with procedural audio chimes and XP progression taps into intrinsic motivation (curiosity and achievement), significantly reducing early onboarding drop-offs.

### 3. Immediate Tactile Gratification (Theme Shifting)
Every class selection dynamically morphs the entire interface—background radial lighting, glowing accents, badge borders, and card highlights. This instantaneous feedback loop signals agency and responsiveness, reinforcing that the platform is dynamic and alive.

### 4. Peak-End Rule & Viral Social Signaling
According to Daniel Kahneman’s Peak-End Rule, users judge an experience by its peak and its conclusion:
- **The Peak**: Testing reflexes in the 20-second **Trial of the Runes** arcade challenge.
- **The End**: Receiving a certified, personalized **Guild Adventurer Pass** with a direct Share on X integration, converting onboarding into organic community viral growth.

### 5. Brand Harmony with Start.ng Ethos
Start.ng's foundational promise is **Good vibes. Good games. Built by the community.** Starting that journey with a standard enterprise form creates brand dissonance; starting with an adventure immediately delivers on the platform's playful promise within the first 60 seconds.

## 🎮 Core Features & Mechanics
- **Quest Map Navigation**: 5-waypoint expedition path (Origin Gate, Class Awakening, Talent Relics, Trial of Runes, Guild Oath).
- **Dynamic Character Classes**: 5 tech archetypes (Code Sorcerer, Pixel Alchemist, Realm Architect, Cyber Sentinel, Data Oracle) with live profile theme-shifting.
- **Start.ng Avatar Forge**: Live avatar customizer conforming to start.ng character standards (ody, hair, hat, ccessory, skinColor, primaryColor).
- **Trial of the Runes (Playable Mini-Game)**: 20-second arcade initiation trial on HTML5 Canvas. Catch falling class runes (+10 XP) and dodge bug glitches.
- **Guild Adventurer Pass**: Certified initiation card with hero handle, class archetype, trial score, and Share on X.
- **Procedural Synthesizer Audio**: Web Audio API engine providing tactile sound cues with zero external audio assets.
